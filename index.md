# Perio360 — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 8, 2026**

---

## Overview

Perio360 is a clinical decision-support tool for periodontal and peri-implant disease classification. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

---

## What data the app collects

Perio360 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or clinical information.

All data you enter — including case identifiers, patient initials, exam dates, and clinical classification results — is stored exclusively on your device using local storage. This data is never sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

The app does not require account registration, login, or an internet connection to function.

---

## Data stored on your device

The following information may be stored locally on your device if you choose to enter it:

- Case identifier or patient reference (optional, user-defined)
- Clinician initials (optional)
- Exam date
- Clinical inputs and classification results generated during use

All locally stored data is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

---

## Data we do not collect

- No names, dates of birth, or government-issued identifiers
- No contact information
- No location data
- No camera or microphone data
- No usage analytics or crash reports
- No advertising identifiers

---

## Third-party services

Perio360 does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

---

## Children's privacy

This application is intended for use by healthcare professionals and students. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from minors.

---

## Clinical disclaimer

Perio360 is intended for educational and decision-support purposes only. It does not define standards of care and is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment. No clinical data entered into this application should be considered a securely stored medical record.

---

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the application evolves. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
