# Perio360 — Support

**Perio360** is a clinical decision-support tool for periodontal and peri-implant disease classification based on the 2017 World Workshop framework.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the app store or transmit patient data?**  
No. All data entered remains on your device and is never sent to any server or third party. See the full [Privacy Policy](../index.md).

**Does the app require an internet connection?**  
No. Perio360 works entirely offline.

**Which classification framework does the app follow?**  
The 2017 World Workshop on the Classification of Periodontal and Peri-Implant Diseases and Conditions (published 2018 in the Journal of Periodontology and Journal of Clinical Periodontology).

**Is this app intended for clinical diagnosis?**  
Perio360 is a decision-support and educational tool. It does not define standards of care and is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment.

**Which iPhone models are supported?**  
Perio360 runs on any iPhone with iOS 16 or later.
